BoardSight privacy policy

Updated October 3, 2026

BoardSight is a chess training website. This policy explains how information is used when you play.

Website hosting

Our hosting provider, Railway, processes information needed to deliver and operate the website, including IP addresses, requested pages, browser information, timestamps and technical request logs. This processing is separate from optional analytics.

Optional Microsoft Clarity analytics

Where consent is required, we only enable analytics after you accept. Elsewhere, analytics are enabled unless you opt out. we use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use BoardSight and improve usability. Clarity can collect clicks or taps, scrolling, session replays, page URLs, timestamps, browser and device information, and network information such as your IP address. Clarity uses cookies to associate visits. Information is sent to and processed by Microsoft.

We do not request advertising consent and send Microsoft a denied advertising-storage choice. Microsoft handles received information under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. See also Clarity's privacy and retention information. Ordinary Clarity recordings are generally available for 30 days; favorite recordings can be retained longer under Microsoft's retention rules.

Regional privacy controls

We request a country code from Cloudflare to decide whether to show a consent prompt. Cloudflare receives your IP address and ordinary request information for this lookup. We do not request GPS location or save your IP address for this feature. If the lookup fails, we show the consent prompt. IP-based location can be approximate, especially with a VPN. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Your choices

Clarity stays off until we have determined your approximate country. For visitors in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, or when the country cannot be determined, Clarity does not load unless you accept analytics. Elsewhere, it loads by default unless you reject analytics or send a Global Privacy Control signal. Rejecting analytics does not prevent playing. We remember your choice on this device for up to six months using local browser storage. You can reopen Analytics settings to change it. Withdrawing consent stops further collection after the page reloads and clears accessible Clarity cookies on this site; it does not automatically delete data previously sent to Microsoft. We honor a browser Global Privacy Control signal by keeping Clarity off.

Younger visitors

BoardSight is not specifically directed at children or teenagers. Visitors under 18 should reject analytics. If you are under 18, please turn off analytics using Analytics settings.
